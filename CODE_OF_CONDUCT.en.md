# Code of conduct

[Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

This platform exists so that disagreements stay peaceful. The same spirit applies to this repository.

- We criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No insults, threats, harassment or discrimination.
- When you disagree, restate what the other person said before you answer.
- No partisan debate in issues: the project supports no party and no candidate.

Reporting: lettre@lafranceendebat.fr. Maintainers may moderate, close a discussion or remove a person.
